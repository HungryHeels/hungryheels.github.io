        Before you go

        Good to know

        Driving
        Cayman drives on the left. Seven Mile Beach is a short drive from Owen Roberts Airport (GCM).
        Money
        The Cayman dollar is pegged at CI$1 = US$1.20. US dollars are widely accepted; check which currency a menu is priced in.
        Sundays
        Brunch is a Cayman tradition and many places are quieter. Book Luca's Sunday brunch well ahead — reservations are essential.
        Sea life
        At Starfish Point, look but keep the starfish in the water. Reef-safe sunscreen is a good idea everywhere.

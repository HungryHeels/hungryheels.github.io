# Draft emails asking Sweetgreen and Chipotle for permission

Both restaurants' terms of use forbid copying or republishing content from their sites without written permission
(see `docs/RESTAURANTS.md`). These drafts ask for it. Nothing here has been sent: send them yourself, from your own
email, to the contact route each company's own terms of use give for questions:

- **Sweetgreen:** hello@sweetgreen.com (Terms of Use §18.7, “Questions, Complaints, Claims”).
- **Chipotle:** the form at https://www.chipotle.com/contact-us (the “Contact support” link in its Terms of Use page).

Fill in your name. Keep the reply. If either says yes, record it in that restaurant's `source.json` as `"permission"`
(who granted it and the date) and follow `docs/RESTAURANTS.md`. If the reply limits what you may use (for example
nutrition but not allergens, or a credit line), follow the limits and note them there too. A “no” or no reply means
the data stays out.

---

**To:** hello@sweetgreen.com
**Subject:** Permission request: using Sweetgreen's published nutrition and allergen information in a free student meal-planning tool

Hello,

I'm a UNC-Chapel Hill student who runs Hungry Heels (https://hungryheels.github.io/), a free, non-commercial,
unofficial website that helps students pick a meal that fits their diet and allergies. It currently covers the
campus dining halls, and I'd like to add the Sweetgreen on Franklin Street.

Your Terms of Use don't allow copying or republishing content from your site, so I'm asking first. May I show
Sweetgreen's published nutrition and allergen information (calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates, allergens and
your cross-contact statements) for your menu ingredients in the site?

If you agree, I would:
- copy the values exactly as you publish them, with the source, date and a link back to your page;
- show your own allergen and cross-contact statements, and tell users to confirm allergies with your staff;
- never use your logo, colors or trade dress, and state clearly that the site is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sweetgreen;
- not sell anything or show ads;
- update or remove the data whenever you ask.

Could you reply in writing to say whether this is OK, and with any conditions (for example a required credit line)? If someone else at Sweetgreen handles requests like this, I'd be grateful if you could pass it on.

Thank you for considering it.

Best,
[Your name]
